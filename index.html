FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Gymman? +

Gymman is a fitness tracking platform designed to help you log workouts, monitor progress and manage useful health metrics.

Can I track my workouts? +

Yes. You can record exercises, muscle groups, sets and reps and review your training progress.

Can I track BMI and calories? +

Yes. Gymman includes BMI and calorie tracking tools connected to your personal account.

Can I create a diet plan? +

Yes. You can save breakfast, lunch, dinner and water intake through your personal diet planner.
